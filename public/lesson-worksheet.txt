3. Check, correct, take away
After both first attempts, open the separate checking guide. Compare details, not just how polished your writing sounds. Keep the first attempt and correction separate.
What I corrected, what remains uncertain, and what help I needed:
My portable method
Goal: name who the output is for and what they should be able to do.
Safe input: use harmless facts; leave out private details. AI is optional.
Draft: make a first version manually or ask for one. Treat it as unverified.
Independent check: compare each claim with the original; recalculate numbers without trusting the draft.
Correct: fix conflicts; label missing information and ask rather than invent.
Decide: keep, revise or do not use it. Review permission before sending anything.
Do not use this exercise as professional advice. For private, high-stakes or safety-critical decisions, stop and use an appropriate qualified person or authoritative source. Choosing not to use AI is a valid skill.
