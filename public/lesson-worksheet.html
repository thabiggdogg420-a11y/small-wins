My portable method

  1. Goal: name who the output is for and what they should be able to do.
  2. Safe input: use harmless facts; leave out private details. AI is optional.
  3. Draft: make a first version manually or ask for one. Treat it as unverified.
  4. Independent check: compare each claim with the original; recalculate numbers without trusting the draft.
  5. Correct: fix conflicts; label missing information and ask rather than invent.
  6. Decide: keep, revise or do not use it. Review permission before sending anything.

Do not use this exercise as professional advice. For private, high-stakes or safety-critical decisions, stop and use an appropriate qualified person or authoritative source. Choosing not to use AI is a valid skill.
