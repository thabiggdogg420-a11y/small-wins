1. Open an AI and make a clear request

Choose a provider you already use, or its available free route: ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. These links contain no lesson answers or personal information. Sign-in and free limits vary; do not buy a plan for this lesson.

  1. Keep this lesson open. In the provider's other tab, start a new chat and locate the message box.
  2. Read the prompt below. Copy it, paste it into that message box, then press the provider's Send button yourself. The provider receives what you paste under its own terms.
  3. Wait for its response, select/copy the answer, return here and paste it below. If you hit a limit, pause or use the worksheet; do not enter a payment or API key.

Notice the prompt supplies the goal, facts, output format and constraints. Those are reusable skills, not magic words.

Enter the claimed whole-pack totals, not the per-item prices. This step makes you read the answer before seeing the check.

2. Check, then ask a useful follow-up

These checks compare only the numbers you entered, not the full AI response. Read its reasoning too: did it round UP to whole packs, distinguish unit price from checkout price and avoid inventing discounts?

If the original is right, do not invent a mistake. Ask why the cheaper unit can cost more at checkout. If it is unclear or wrong, ask for the missing reasoning or correction. Read the exact prompt before manually copying it to the SAME AI conversation.

3. Try a changed task without the template

New fictional task: you need 14 identical items. A has 8 per pack for 5.60. B has 5 per pack for 3.75. Compare the unit prices AND the whole-pack checkout costs. Same currency and quality; no delivery or discounts.

The earlier answers are hidden to give you space to try. Write your own request, send it in a new AI chat, then bring back its answer. You can ask for help, but record it honestly.

This records an attempt, not proven competence, independent transfer or money saved. If a check is unresolved, keep that uncertainty in your record. For your next real comparison, check current source prices and any extra costs before deciding. Use your own public offers: prepare a prompt, ask your AI and check its totals.
