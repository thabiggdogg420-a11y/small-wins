1. Predict before calculating

Fictional example: you need 10 identical items. A has 6 per pack for 4.20. B has 10 per pack for 7.50. Which costs less at checkout? Try your own numbers after this example.

Convert first: 500 g = 0.5 kg; 750 mL = 0.75 litres. Do not compare grams with kilograms, or different product qualities. Use one currency and tax-inclusive pack prices. Delivery, discounts and deposits are not included. Use up to six decimal places for quantities and two for prices; use a dot, not a comma.
