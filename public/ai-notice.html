1. Start with the original


2. Ask your AI, then bring back its answer
Open a provider you already use or an available free route: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Start a new chat, review/copy the prompt below, paste into its message box and send it yourself. Copy its answer back here. Provider terms and limits apply; do not buy a subscription for this exercise. If blocked, keep your original and return later.
The prompt gives a goal, a source, an output shape and boundaries. Notice why the source matters: AI cannot reliably guess a missing fee or deadline.


3. Build your checked action sheet
Read the source and the AI answer above. Add each useful action yourself, correcting it as needed. Put missing fees, contact details or dates under Unresolved question, not the action list. Event date and reply deadline can be different. Nothing is extracted or judged by AI on this page.
A source-backed action needs an exact quote. We check that those words occur in your source, not that they mean what the action says. You must compare the meaning, dates, numbers and who is responsible. A matching quote is not proof.


The AI invented something or I am still unsure
Write the specific issue. Prepare a follow-up to the same AI, review it, then manually copy it. It may still be wrong. Keep unresolved issues as questions rather than forcing a confident answer.
After checking its reply, correct your action sheet and run the evidence check again. The download keeps both answers and your check. This records your review, not proof that AI is right. If you cannot get a reply, remove the optional follow-up and keep unresolved issues as questions. Removing it asks before erasing its drafts.


4. Keep your reviewed next actions
This is a learner-reviewed draft, not proof of learning or a completed real-world task. To practise again, choose a different harmless notice and try writing the request yourself. Nothing is sent or scheduled for you.
Keep a download first if you want a record. Downloaded copies are not deleted by clearing this page.
