One possible first response
• At a volunteer seed-packing session, I copied seed names and dates from the organiser's list onto envelopes and compared the labels with the list. I set aside two envelopes with unreadable dates for the organiser.
• When asked about seeds for a shady plot, I passed the question to the coordinator rather than giving growing advice.

Check: T01 labels the activity accurately; T02 supports copying, comparison, two unresolved envelopes and referral; T03 supports referring the visitor's question. Exactly two bullets and fewer than 65 words. It does not imply repeated shifts or expertise.

One possible revised response
At a volunteer seed-packing session, I counted the remaining envelopes and recorded the count on the supply sheet. I set aside envelopes with unreadable dates for the organiser to resolve and passed a visitor's growing question to the coordinator rather than giving advice.

Check: one paragraph, fewer than 55 words; T01/T04 support the supply-recording focus, T02/T03 support handling uncertainty. Removing the detailed labelling process makes room for the changed priority. No total inventory, accuracy rate or qualification is inferred.

Example correction request, if a draft said “My horticultural expertise improved stock accuracy by 20%”
Remove the expertise and 20% claims: T05 supplies no qualification and T04 supplies no improvement measure. Describe counting remaining envelopes and recording the count instead. Use one paragraph of at most 55 words and keep uncertainty handling supported by T02/T03. Do not add availability.
