OPTIONAL HINTS — RECORD HINT HELP

Use only as much help as you need. Keep your first attempt unchanged if you already wrote it. Closing this page does not undo having seen it.

1. Separate the requested format from the evidence. Count your bullet points and words yourself. A job ad's requirement is not evidence that an applicant already has a skill.
2. Underline action verbs in the facts. Compare them with verbs in your draft. Did you turn participation into employment, copying into expertise, or referring a question into giving advice?
3. On the changed request, ask which supplied fact deals with remaining supplies. Decide what to remove to make room. Do not invent a starting quantity or improvement.
4. For a correction request, name one unsupported phrase, the source that limits it and the specific change required. Then inspect the revised text again. Polished wording is not a factual check.

Return to your saved draft. If you need an answer example, choose checks explicitly and record worked-answer help. There is no automatic grade.
